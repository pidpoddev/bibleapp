## Purpose

Give girls a cozy set of prayer-journal stickers for night entries, quiet time, and answered-prayer pages.

## Art Direction

- Puffy sticker shapes.
- Cream, blush, sky blue, sage, dusty rose, and warm gold.
- Large readable words.
- Simple icons that survive the 52px sticker picker.
- No thin line art, tiny page writing, or detailed scene drawings.
