## Purpose

Give girls a comforting encouragement pack for hard-day prayers, brave journal pages, Daily Devotional notes, and verse art.

## Art Direction

- AI-generated puffy sticker artwork.
- Cream, blush, lavender, mint, sky blue, sage, dusty rose, and warm gold.
- Large readable words.
- Gentle hearts, shields, clouds, stars, sparkles, and small faith accents.
- No guilt, fear, shame, pressure language, or mental-health overclaims.

## QA
