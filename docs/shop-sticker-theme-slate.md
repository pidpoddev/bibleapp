# Faith Canvas Sticker Theme Slate

Project manager recommendation: build sticker themes that map to actual journaling moments, not just generic faith nouns. Girls should immediately know where they would place the stickers: prayer pages, Bible study notes, church notes, hard-day pages, gratitude pages, and verse art.

## Top Themes To Build First

### 1. Prayer Night

Priority: P0

Why it works: Prayer journaling is one of the clearest Faith Canvas use cases, and a cozy night theme gives girls a reason to decorate repeated entries.

Style: Puffy cream, blush, sky blue, warm gold. Moon, stars, hearts, soft clouds, cozy labels.

Sticker ideas:

- `prayer time`
- `dear God`
- `amen`
- moon with tiny cross
- cloud heart
- folded hands simplified as a heart icon
- prayer list label
- answered prayer label
- cozy Bible
- mug with heart
- star cluster
- sleepy sparkle bow

Build notes: Keep stars large and sparse. Avoid tiny handwritten text.

### 2. Bible Study Besties

Priority: P0

Why it works: Study supplies are familiar and cute. It connects faith with notebooks, markers, tabs, and the way girls already decorate school materials.

Style: Cute desk-supply stickers with faith touches. Bold shapes, no small page lines.

Sticker ideas:

- puffy Bible tab
- highlighter
- pencil with heart
- `study notes`
- `favorite verse`
- `read + pray`
- bookmark cross
- sticky note heart
- desk mug
- open notebook icon without lines
- `what stood out`
- `God's word`
- verse card
- bow paperclip

Build notes: This should be a strong second pack because it has clear utility in Bible Study and Studio.

### 3. Brave Girl Faith

Priority: P0

Why it works: Encouragement stickers are useful on hard-day pages and feel personal. This can be both cute and emotionally meaningful.

Style: Bold word stickers with hearts, shields, bows, clouds, and small crosses.

Approved words:

- `brave`
- `loved`
- `chosen`
- `not alone`
- `God is near`
- `be still`
- `keep going`
- `grace today`
- `take heart`
- `God with me`
- `gentle strength`
- `shine on`

Build notes: Use AI-generated artwork for final stickers, then QA every visible word against the approved copy. Avoid pressure language like `no excuses`, `try harder`, or anything shame-based.

### 4. Hearts And Halos

Priority: P1

Why it works: Decorative heart packs are easy to use everywhere. This is the pack girls would reach for when a page needs to feel cute but not over-themed.

Style: Puffed hearts, tiny halos, bows, sparkles, soft crosses, simple flowers.

Sticker ideas:

- halo heart
- cross heart
- sparkle heart
- bow heart
- flower heart
- `love`
- `grace`
- `joy`
- `blessed`
- `Jesus loves me`
- heart cluster
- tiny faith charm
- pastel stamp heart
- ribbon heart
- cloud heart
- gold star heart

Build notes: Use mostly icons with a few short words. This should be very readable.

### 5. Sunday Notes

Priority: P1

Why it works: Church Day has its own workflow, and girls may want supplies that feel different from daily prayer pages.

Style: Sunday morning but not formal. Sunrise, note labels, worship hearts, cocoa/coffee, Bible bag, soft ribbons.

Sticker ideas:

- `sermon notes`
- `Sunday`
- `amen`
- `worship`
- sunrise cross
- church notes label
- Bible bag
- cocoa cup with cross
- heart music note
- `key verse`
- `remember this`
- `pray this week`
- pastel ribbon tab
- starburst cross

Build notes: Avoid detailed church buildings. They will collapse at picker size.

### 6. Thankful Heart

Priority: P1

Why it works: Gratitude is a repeated journaling habit, and thankful stickers make ordinary pages feel intentional.

Style: Warm cream, blush, coral, sage, gold. Hearts, labels, little gifts, simple flowers.

Sticker ideas:

- `thankful`
- `gratitude`
- `answered`
- `today I noticed`
- `little blessing`
- `thank you God`
- heart bouquet
- gift heart
- sunshine heart
- thankful label
- prayer ribbon
- joy stamp

Build notes: Good candidate for a matching background pack later.

## Secondary Themes

### 7. Soft School Faith

Priority: P2

Why it works: School is a daily context for girls. This theme can connect faith with tests, friendships, courage, and ordinary routines.

Sticker ideas:

- backpack heart
- pencil cross
- `before the test`
- `be kind`
- `brave today`
- `friend prayer`
- notebook heart
- lunch note
- `God with me`
- locker bow
- study star
- `peace please`

Build notes: No school names, no identity details, no social-pressure language.

### 8. Verse Card Cuties

Priority: P2

Why it works: Supports Studio and favorite verse art. These are decorative frames and labels more than standalone stickers.

Sticker ideas:

- `favorite verse`

- `memory verse`
- heart corner tabs
- ribbon label
- sparkle underline
- soft quote bubble
- mini frame cross
- cloud label

Build notes: Needs testing in Studio because these may be larger than normal stickers.

### 9. Cozy Devotional Desk

Priority: P2

Why it works: Girls like desk/stationery aesthetics, and the app already has a quiet journaling tone.

Sticker ideas:

Why it works: Good for church, youth group, and worship notes, but can wait until core journal packs are stronger.

Sticker ideas:

- `worship`
- `hallelujah`

- simple microphone
- worship sparkle
- raised-heart icon
- stage light heart

- `God is good`

Build notes: Avoid music staff lines and detailed hands.

## Project Manager Verdict

Start with these three:

1. `Prayer Night`
2. `Bible Study Besties`
3. `Brave Girl Faith`

Reason: Together they cover the three strongest repeat-use needs: prayer journaling, Bible study, and encouragement. They are also easier to make readable than detailed scene-based stickers.

Do not build another generic cross/dove/Bible pack until the shop has more lifestyle and journaling-specific supplies. Faith symbols should be accents inside useful sticker themes, not the whole product strategy.
