Faith Canvas Support

For app help, privacy questions, child-safety concerns, or deletion requests, contact support@pidpod.com.

What to include

  • The device type, such as iPhone, iPad, or Android.
  • The app version if visible in the app or store listing.
  • A short description of what happened.
  • For Cloud Save help, the Cloud Save username or recovery details shown in the app.

Do not email a child's Secret Phrase, password, home address, school name, or private journal text.

Delete Cloud Save data

To request deletion of Cloud Save records, email support@pidpod.com with the Cloud Save username or recovery details available in the app. We may need to verify the request before deleting data.
