Faith Canvas Privacy Policy

Faith Canvas is designed to help kids and families read Bible verses, journal, create verse art, and save reflections. This policy explains what information the app uses, why it is used, and how parents or guardians can contact us. This policy applies to the Faith Canvas mobile app and the Faith Canvas pages at pidpod.com/faithcanvas. Faith Canvas is provided by PidPod, the applications brand of Bumfuzzle Inc., a Tennessee corporation.

Short version

Faith Canvas does not sell personal information, does not use third-party advertising, and does not include public chat or social posting. In the current launch build, journal content, favorites, verse art, and settings stay on the device unless a family chooses to export data themselves.

Information the app may store or collect

  • Journal entries, prayer notes, favorite verses, verse designs, app themes, and shop/customization choices created in the app.
  • Basic technical server logs, such as IP address, request time, and error information, when the app contacts PidPod services.
  • Support messages sent by email, including the contact information included in that email.
  • Faith Canvas may ask for photo library permission only if the user chooses to save verse art or journal images to the device.
  • Faith Canvas does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising tracking permissions for its core journaling features.

Optional Shop purchases

Faith Canvas is free to download. Families may optionally buy one-time sticker packs. Purchases are processed by Apple on the App Store or by Google on Google Play. Purchase status is validated by RevenueCat so a family can restore packs they already bought on the same store account. This may include a device or purchase identifier needed to restore those packs. Faith Canvas does not collect a child email address for purchases and does not use advertising tracking.

How information is used

  • To save and reopen the user's journals, favorites, settings, and verse-art creations.
  • To troubleshoot app problems, answer support requests, and keep the service reliable.
  • To comply with legal, safety, platform, and security obligations.

Cloud Save and encryption

Cloud Save is not enabled in the current App Store and Google Play launch build. Faith Canvas keeps saved app content on the device unless a family chooses to export data themselves. A future parent-approved Cloud Save flow may be added later and would be disclosed before release.

Families should avoid writing sensitive personal details in journal entries, including home addresses, school names, phone numbers, passwords, or private family information.

Children's privacy

Faith Canvas is intended to be age-appropriate for kids and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parent or guardian consent. Children should ask a parent or guardian before using Cloud Save or contacting support.

Parents and guardians may request review, deletion, or help with a child's information by emailing support@pidpod.com.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not use third-party advertising in Faith Canvas. We may share limited information only when needed to operate the service, respond to support requests, protect safety/security, comply with law, or work with service providers that help host or maintain PidPod services.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on the user's device until it is deleted in the app or the app is removed. The current launch build does not send journal content to Cloud Save.

The app includes a local reset control in Settings. Support and deletion questions can be sent to support@pidpod.com. Do not send private journal text, passwords, home addresses, school names, or other sensitive details by email.

Security

Faith Canvas uses reasonable safeguards intended to protect saved data. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, so families should avoid writing sensitive personal details in journal entries.

International users

Faith Canvas and PidPod services are operated by Bumfuzzle Inc., a Tennessee corporation, from the United States. If the app is used outside the United States, information may be processed in the United States or other places where service providers operate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Privacy, support, and deletion requests can be sent to support@pidpod.com. Faith Canvas is provided by PidPod, the applications brand of Bumfuzzle Inc., a Tennessee corporation.
