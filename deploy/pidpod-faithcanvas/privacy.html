Faith Canvas Privacy Policy

Faith Canvas is designed to help kids and families read Bible verses, journal, create verse art, and save reflections. This policy explains what information the app uses, why it is used, and how parents or guardians can contact us.

Short version

Faith Canvas does not sell personal information, does not use third-party advertising, and does not include public chat or social posting. Journal content is stored on the device by default. Optional Cloud Save is designed to send encrypted saved content to PidPod servers so it can be restored on another device.

Information the app may store or collect

  • Journal entries, prayer notes, favorite verses, verse designs, app themes, and shop/customization choices created in the app.
  • Optional Cloud Save username, device identifier, recovery identifier, timestamps, and encrypted backup records when Cloud Save is turned on.
  • Basic technical server logs, such as IP address, request time, and error information, when the app contacts PidPod services.
  • Support messages sent by email, including the contact information included in that email.

How information is used

  • To save and reopen the user's journals, favorites, settings, and verse-art creations.
  • To provide optional encrypted Cloud Save and restore across devices.
  • To troubleshoot app problems, answer support requests, and keep the service reliable.
  • To comply with legal, safety, platform, and security obligations.

Cloud Save and encryption

Cloud Save is optional. When enabled, saved app content is encrypted before it is sent to PidPod servers. The app uses the user's Secret Phrase to help protect that content. PidPod is not designed to read the plain text of encrypted journal entries stored through Cloud Save.

A parent or guardian should help a child decide whether to use Cloud Save and should keep the Secret Phrase in a safe place. If the phrase is lost, encrypted content may not be recoverable.

Children's privacy

Faith Canvas is intended to be age-appropriate for kids and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parent or guardian consent. Children should ask a parent or guardian before using Cloud Save or contacting support.

Parents and guardians may request review, deletion, or help with a child's information by emailing support@pidpod.com.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not use third-party advertising in Faith Canvas. We may share limited information only when needed to operate the service, respond to support requests, protect safety/security, comply with law, or work with service providers that help host or maintain PidPod services.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on the user's device until it is deleted in the app or the app is removed. Optional Cloud Save data remains on PidPod systems until it is deleted through app controls or a verified support request.

To request deletion, email support@pidpod.com with the Cloud Save username or recovery details available in the app. Do not send a child's Secret Phrase by email.

Security

Faith Canvas uses reasonable safeguards intended to protect saved data, including encrypted Cloud Save records. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, so families should avoid writing sensitive personal details in journal entries.

International users

Faith Canvas and PidPod services are operated from the United States. If the app is used outside the United States, information may be processed in the United States or other places where service providers operate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Privacy, support, and deletion requests can be sent to support@pidpod.com.
