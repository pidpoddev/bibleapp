## Description (App Store and Play)

Faith Canvas is a gentle Bible journaling app for kids and families.

Read public-domain Bible text, save favorite verses, write prayer and study notes, and turn a verse into soft art with stickers, backgrounds, and colors.

The app is private. There are no ads, public profiles, followers, chat, or a social feed. Journals, favorites, verse art, and settings stay on this device in the launch build unless you export them yourself.

Optional Shop packs are one-time sticker sets for decorating journals and verse art. Restore Purchases is available in the Shop if you reinstall the app on the same store account.

Photo library access is requested only if you choose to save an image to your device.

## In-App Settings Summary

## Data Safety Notes

- Core use stores journal entries, prayer notes, favorite verses, verse designs, themes, and app settings locally on the device.
- Cloud Save is off in the launch build, so the app does not send journal content to PidPod servers through Cloud Save.
- Optional Shop purchases send purchase status to Apple or Google and RevenueCat so packs can be restored.
- Support email may include whatever contact information and app details the sender chooses to provide.
- Basic technical server logs may be created when the app contacts PidPod services.
- Faith Canvas may ask for photo library permission only if the user chooses to save verse art or journal images to the device.
- Faith Canvas does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising tracking permissions for its core journaling features.
- Do not ask families to email a password, home address, school name, or private journal text.

## Deletion Copy

Families can reset local journal data in Settings. For support or privacy questions, email `support@pidpod.com`. Do not email private journal text, passwords, home addresses, school names, or other sensitive details.

## Child Safety Copy

Faith Canvas is not a social network. It does not include public profiles, public comments, followers, open chat, or public posting. Children should ask a parent or guardian before contacting support.
